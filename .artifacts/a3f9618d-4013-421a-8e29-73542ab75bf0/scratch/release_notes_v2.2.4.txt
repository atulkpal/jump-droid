# Jump Droid v2.2.4 — Elite Urgency & Global Delivery

### Added — Native "Elite" Sales & Urgency
- **Dynamic Best-Offer Selection**: Updated PurchaseManager.kt to automatically select the lowest price from Google Play offers.
- **Subtle Urgency Mode**: Smart countdown timer for sales ending in less than 3 days.
- **Native Discount Detection**: Integration with Play Store metadata for official percentage labels.

### Improved — Unified "Elite Upgrade" Flow
- **Universal Dialog**: Shared EliteUpgradeDialog and EliteBenefitsList for UI consistency.
- **Functional CTA Overhaul**: Clickable premium hints and functional "GO PREMIUM" buttons.
- **Fixed Footer Layout**: Redesigned Settings screen with a fixed bottom container for accessibility.

### Changed — Background Notification Recovery
- **Runtime Permissions**: POST_NOTIFICATIONS flow for Android 13+.
- **Global Topic Subscription**: Automatic subscription to the "all" FCM topic.
- **Diagnostic Logging**: Explicit FCM token and subscription status logging.

### Fixed — Engine Safety & Rendering Stability
- **RadialGradient Crash Fix**: Three-tier safety system to prevent NaN values in the native Skia shader engine.
- **Physics Stability**: Clamped dt for stable calculations during frame spikes.
