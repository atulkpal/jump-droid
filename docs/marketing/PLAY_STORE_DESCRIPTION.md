## Ascend Beyond the Atmosphere

Pilot a droid-controlled rocket through increasingly hostile atmospheric layers in **Jump Droid**, a precision vertical exploration simulator built for those who value skill, strategy, and discovery.

### EXPLORE THE UNKNOWN
Journey through 12 unique atmospheric zones, from the vibrant morning skies of Earth to the reality-warping depths of **The Singularity**. Each zone features distinct environmental hazards, procedural platforming, and dynamic soundtracks that evolve with your altitude.

### BUILD YOUR ASCENSION
Your rocket isn't just a vehicle—it’s a modular "Build." Customize your strategy with 4 distinct rocket classes and a registry of 17 unlockable modules. Manage critical systems like **Fuel, Heat, Shields, and Hull Integrity** as you navigate the thin line between progress and catastrophe.

### MASTER THE FLOW
Harness the power of the **Combo System**. Chain landings on diverse platform types—Flux, Graviton, and more—to trigger survival rewards and keep your climb alive. 

### NEW: ZEN MODE PROTOCOL
Need a break from the chaos? Activate the **Zen Mode Protocol** for a pressure-free exploration experience. Toggle between unlocked soundtracks and ascend through the stars without threats or bosses.

### KEY FEATURES:
- **Tactical Boss Encounters**: Defeat 11 massive bosses with multi-phase AI and destructible weak points.
- **Deep Codex System**: Uncover 48 unique narrative logs and collect rare artifacts to piece together the history of "The Great Signal."
- **High-Fidelity HUD**: Stay informed with a technical "digital readout" aesthetic and satisfying unlock notifications.
- **Prestige & Eternal Mode**: Push beyond the 100km boundary and reset your origin for permanent stat multipliers.

**Hard but Fair.** Jump Droid is a skill-based journey where every meter earned is a testament to your mastery.
