# Jump Droid v2.0.0 — Production Ascension Release Notes

**Release Date:** July 27, 2026
**Version Code:** 7

## Overview
Jump Droid v2.0.0 marks the transition from Beta to full Production. This release culminates the "Fleet Ascension" milestone, introducing the complete rocket fleet, a robust cosmetic economy, and a massive visual overhaul of the Earth zone.

## Key Features

### 1. Fleet Ascension & Mastery
- **Complete Fleet**: Unlock and master the **Scout**, **Tank**, and **Experimental** rocket classes, each with unique handling and module capacities.
- **Mastery System**: Earn Mastery Points (MP) through discoveries, artifacts, and zone exploration. Rise through 5 distinct procedural ranks.

### 2. Living World Visuals
- **Earth Overhaul**: Experience the new "Morning City" backdrop featuring ice-capped mountains, dynamic city traffic, and atmospheric aviation lights.
- **Cyber-Packet UI**: Scoring events now manifest as physical "Cyber-Packets" that fly into your HUD.
- **Enhanced FX**: Total visual pass on all 12 bosses, engine thrusters, and environmental particles.

### 3. Tactical HUD & Flight Log
- **Non-Blocking Notifications**: Stay focused on the flight. Achievements and unlocks now settle into a pulsating "Flight Log" deck on your HUD.
- **Post-Run Data**: Review detailed expedition statistics on the revamped Game Over screen.

### 4. Cosmetic Economy
- **The Hangar Shop**: Spend your hard-earned Jump Credits (JC) on exclusive engine trails (Solar Flare, Void Glitch) and hull coatings (Chrome, Stealth).
- **JC Exchange**: Convert surplus resources into Continue Credits.

### 5. Production Stability
- **Online Leaderboards**: Restored Global Terminal functionality via secure Firebase Auth integration.
- **Performance**: Silky smooth 60fps even in the Void, thanks to geometric path caching and optimized noise shaders.
- **Reset Protocols**: Reliable Soft and Hard reset options for profile management.
